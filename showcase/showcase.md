# Carom

Two paddles. Two turning obstacles. A ball that refuses to fly straight. Carom
is a top-down duel where a little movement at the right moment changes the
whole rally. Take on the computer in Solo, or bring a friend to the same
keyboard for Versus.

The opening serve gives you a moment to settle in. Meet it squarely for a clean
return, or slide through the contact to load the ball with spin. Watch its pale
trail curl across the dark court as your opponent scrambles to read the bend.

The gold bars never sit still: they sway and rotate, opening lanes and closing
them again. A dangerous obstruction can become your best bank shot. Thread a
gap, ricochet off a tilted face, or turn a defensive return into an unexpected
angle behind the other paddle.

Every paddle hit picks up the pace. First to eleven takes the match, but only
with a two-point lead, so a close finish can keep going well past match point.
Keep your nerve, keep moving, and make the next angle yours.
